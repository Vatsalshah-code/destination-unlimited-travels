Privacy Notice & Client Consent

Data Protection & Confidentiality Commitment
🔒 Sensitive Document Protection Notice:

We treat your passport, Aadhaar, financial records, and personal identity documents with the utmost confidentiality. Your documents are saved strictly into an isolated, private file storage folder designated solely for your application. They are never stored in public directories and are accessible exclusively to authorized case officers for the sole purpose of visa and passport filing.

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, your records are subject to strict audit logging and can be deleted upon request once processing is finalized.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions by Applicants
How are my sensitive documents protected?
All uploaded files (Passport, Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Statements) are immediately stored in an isolated, private file directory created solely for your client ID. They are never exposed to public links.
What file formats and sizes are allowed?
You can upload PDF documents or clear high-resolution JPG, PNG, and WEBP image files up to 10MB each.
What happens after I submit?
You will instantly receive your unique Client Reference ID and an email confirmation. Your designated case officer will review your documents within 24 business hours.
Can I check my application status later?
Yes! You can visit the Track My Application page at any time using your Client ID and registered phone/email.
